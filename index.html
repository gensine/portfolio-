Zero-knowledge supply chain tracking with zk-SNARKs, Circom, Groth16 & IPFS

Developed a blockchain-based supply chain tracking platform that uses zk-SNARKs to verify product location checkpoints without exposing sensitive GPS coordinates. Integrated Circom and Groth16 for zero-knowledge proof generation, a custom Solidity verifier for on-chain validation, and IPFS for off-chain proof storage.   • Designed QR-based product verification and supply chain checkpoint tracking.
  • Explored applications in pharmaceutical authentication, food safety, and counterfeit prevention.
